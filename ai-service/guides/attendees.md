**Where:** Sidebar → "Registrations" → "Attendees" ([Attendees](/attendees)).

**Requires:** A selected gathering.

**Find:**
1. Use "Search by name or email...".
2. Click "Filter" → "Add filter": Name, Email, Category, Gender, Created, "Has tickets".
3. "Views" saves the setup for the team.

**Export:** "Export CSV", or "Export filtered CSV". It has one row per ticket, with columns Name, Email, Category, Gender, Age group and Product ID. Check-in codes are not exported.

**Edit an attendee:**
1. Click the row, or use ⋮ → "Edit".
2. Click "Edit details".
3. Change "Name", "Email" or "Gender", then click "Save". Age group is read-only.

**Detail page:**
- Tickets: see the grant-swap-remove-ticket guide.
- "Activity": the history of ticket changes.
- Links: "View human" and "View application".

**Gotchas:**
- Attendees appear after an application is accepted or a purchase or grant happens.
- To give free tickets to many people at once, use the "Invite" button on this page. See the bulk-grant-tickets guide.
