**Where:** The "Ask EdgeOS" button in the page header (operator and above). It opens on the right on desktop and fullscreen on mobile. Close it with "–" or Esc.

**What it can do:**
- Answer questions about your data.
- Explain where things are and how to do them in the backoffice.
- Run operations for you: create, update, grant, approve.
- Prepare custom CSV/XLSX exports.

It uses the gathering selected in the sidebar unless you name another gathering in the same organization.

**Approvals:**
1. Reads run automatically.
2. Any change shows a "Review before continuing" card with "Proposed change" and "What will happen".
3. Click the action button to approve, or "Cancel". Nothing changes without your approval.

**Downloads:** Files appear as cards ("File ready to download" / "Generate and download"). The file is generated only when you click.

**Conversations:**
- Conversations are saved to your account.
- The history icon shows "Recent conversations", "New conversation" and "Delete current conversation".
- Use the thumbs up and down buttons to rate answers.

**Gotchas:**
- Viewers and Check In Controllers don't have the assistant.
- AI can make mistakes. Review important information.
